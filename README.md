### About

I'm a full stack developer from Ahmedabad who likes taking things from an idea to a reliable production system. At [Blink Analytics](https://github.com/Blink-Analytics) I build core systems for [Serin](https://serin-ai.com), an AI interview platform: backend services, job queues, real-time infra and the occasional landing page. I'm also studying Computer Engineering at PDEU.

Most of my day is spent working alongside agentic coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex). Outside of code, I trade Indian equities and forex and read too much about markets.

- ⚙️ Running a **Python/FastAPI job queue** I built from scratch, which cut cloud costs by **~70%**
